{{subjectName}} How to book a service?

You can follow the below steps to book a service:

  1. Search for the Service you need.
  2. Open the Service and follow the instructions as you go forward.
  3. Once you have booked the service, wait for Your MaintenanceWala to be assigned. A verified MaintenanceWala will be assigned to you 1 hour before the booked time.
  4. Your MaintenanceWala will reach the address you have provided and deliver the service.

For any further queries, please connect on the helpline number.
